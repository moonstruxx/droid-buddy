# Proposal

## Why

The module UI's faceplates put rotary elements where the hardware does not.
P2B8, P4B2 and P10 big pots, the S10's two rotary switches, and the E4's four
encoders are all modelled side by side in one row; on the real panels each of
those families is a single vertical column. The DB8E's encoder sits to the
right of its buttons instead of below them. The E4/DB8E 32-LED rings are
modelled roughly 2.6× too small — a 9 mm ring against a measured ~24 mm
square — so adjacent rings overlap and a ring does not read as a ring. The
P2B8 case is visible on the current module-UI screenshot.

The cause is recorded in the data itself: the affected
`controller_geometry.json` entries carry `"Assumed: pitches/margins"` notes,
because the generator guessed element positions and pitches where the manual
gave no millimetres. The repo's second geometry source already disagrees:
`rack_geometry.json` declares E4 as `{ "kind": "stack", "count": 4 }` — a
vertical stack — while `controller_geometry.json` lays the four encoders out
in four columns.

## What Changes

- Vertical stacking with measured positions, pitches and sizes for every
  affected rotary family, taken from the manual's faceplate images (scale
  pinned twice per panel: 5 HP = 224 px ↔ 25.4 mm, 6 HP = 225 px ↔ 30.48 mm,
  both confirming 1132 px ↔ 128.5 mm):
  - P2B8 pots, P4B2 pots, P10 big pots, S10 rotary switches: one centred
    column at ~24.8 mm pitch (was a 2-cell row at 10 mm pitch).
  - E4 encoders: one centred column at 27.8 mm pitch (was a 4-cell row at
    7 mm).
  - P10's eight small pots keep their 2-column × 4-row matrix — the declared
    exception — at the measured 12.6 × 15.1 mm pitch.
  - DB8E: encoder and ring move below the button grid (measured button
    centres x 7.9 / 23.1 mm, rows y 41.4 → 86.8 mm; encoder at (15.24,
    108.1) mm), correcting a 33 mm vertical misplacement.
  - P2B8/P4B2 button grid: measured pitch 12.6 × 15.0 mm (was 9.2 × 15.0).
  - E4/DB8E LED rings: 9 LEDs per side at 2.76 mm pitch with 1.8 mm cells — a
    ~23.9 mm square ring — regenerated by a parameterized `ring_32`.
- `tools/acquire_geometry.py` rewritten for those six controller blocks with
  the measured values; the `grids`/`notes` provenance switches from "assumed"
  to measured. `controller_geometry.json` is regenerated by that script.
- Regression coverage: arrangement, position and ring assertions in
  `src/physical.rs` (written first, expected red), plus a cross-file agreement
  test in `src/geometry.rs` tying E4's stack shape to `rack_geometry.json`'s
  `e4` grid — mirroring the existing B32 4×8 agreement test.
- Visual artifacts: `docs/assets/*.png` re-rendered, plus one new harness shot
  over a new fixture that shows all six corrected faceplates at once.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `module-ui`: faceplate element fidelity — a family the panel arranges as one
  vertical column renders as one column at the panel's measured pitch; a
  family the panel arranges as a matrix keeps the panel's rows and columns
  (the P10 small pots stay a 2 × 4 matrix); the DB8E's encoder renders below
  its buttons.
- `physical-scale-model`: new requirement "Controller geometry is measured and
  verified" — the embedded geometry carries each family's real panel
  arrangement and extents rather than assumed pitches, is regenerable from its
  generator, and is verified: cells inside the module rect, grids matching
  token-family counts, and grid shapes shared with `rack_geometry.json`
  agreeing.

## Impact

- Touched: `tools/acquire_geometry.py`, `controller_geometry.json`,
  `src/physical.rs` and `src/geometry.rs` (tests only),
  `tools/screenshots/**`, `docs/assets/*.png`, and a new
  `fixtures/faceplate_rotary_stack.ini`.
- No Rust production-code change: the loader and model match element cells by
  `(family, element number decoded from the label)` and the renderer takes its
  glyph from `HwComponent.kind`, so both are arrangement-agnostic. Only the
  embedded data and the tests change.
- No controller width (HP) change, no rack packing, fold-bar or zoom change;
  every cell still lies inside its module rect, so panel extents are unchanged.
- No new dependencies, no config keys, no persistence, no `.ini` mutation.

## Non-goals

- No S10 rotary-versus-toggle glyph work. `module-ui`'s "S10 distinguishes
  rotary and toggle switches" scenario is not implemented today:
  `cell_visuals` draws one switch glyph for every switch, and the generator's
  `kind: "rotary"` / `"touch"` cell annotations are never read
  (`ElementCellEntry` has no `kind` field). This change carries that scenario
  over verbatim and records the gap as a follow-up, not a fix.
- No change to `rack_geometry.json` itself; only a test now ties E4 to its
  `e4` grid.
- No ARCHITECTURE.md / DESIGN.md hand-edits (derived artifacts, and neither
  states an affected arrangement).
- No keybinding, help, or config changes.
- No re-measurement of the controllers this change does not touch: B32, P8S8,
  M4, G8, X7, master, R2M/R2C.

## New types (YAGNI consumer check)

- None. The change regenerates existing embedded data and adjusts tests: no new
  Rust type, field, variant, or derive.
